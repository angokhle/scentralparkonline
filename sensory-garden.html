Before you visit

Common questions

How do I book?

Download the app — booking is exclusively through The Scentral Park app, for every session type on this page.

Does my dog need to be vaccinated?

Yes, all dogs must be up to date on core vaccinations before their first visit.

Is my dog off-leash the whole time?

Bring your dog in on a leash, then unleash them once inside — the garden is fully fenced and secure.

What's provided, and what should I bring?

We provide water and waste bags. Dry treats are optional to bring — but no toys or outside food are allowed inside the garden.

How often should we visit?

We recommend weekly visits for the best results, though it varies by dog and by how your schedule works.
