Before you book

Common questions

Do I need to do anything before my dog's first stay?

Yes — an Initial Fitment assessment (60–90 minutes, ₹1,500) is required before a first boarding stay, to check comfort and fit with the space.

What if my dog's never boarded before?

Acclimatization sessions ease first-time dogs in gradually, with a half-day or overnight option before a full boarding stay.

How is pricing worked out?

Both overnight boarding and daycare are priced by dog size — small, medium, or large.

Is my dog supervised overnight?

Yes — supervision continues 24/7 for every overnight stay.
