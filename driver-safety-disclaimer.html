Driver Safety Disclaimer

This dog park is located in a village area where there may be local traffic, livestock, and other animals present. For your safety and the safety of others, we urge all visitors to drive cautiously and be aware of potential hazards, including but not limited to:

  • Cattle & livestock: animals may be on or close to roads — give them extra consideration while driving.
  • Village traffic: local traffic conditions, pedestrians, and narrow roads may affect driving speed and visibility.
  • Other animals: small animals, including pets and wildlife, may also be in the vicinity.

To ensure a safe visit for you and your dogs, please travel at a reasonable speed, watch for animals on or near the road, and follow all local traffic rules.

Thank you for your attention, and for driving carefully.

Rooted in science and created with a lot of care and with an in-depth understanding of dog behaviour, we take pride in offering an experience that is unique and dog centric.
