Before you order

Common questions

Do I need to pre-book food?

Yes — pre-booking closes at 5pm the day before your visit.

Is the dog pizza actually safe for dogs?

Yes — it's made on an oat-and-egg base with chicken or fish, built specifically for dogs rather than adapted from the human menu.

Can I add the cafe to a garden session?

Yes — the cafe can be added on to Exclusive or Shared sensory garden slots.
