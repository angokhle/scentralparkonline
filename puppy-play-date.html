Puppy Play Date

A monthly sensory playdate for pups aged 4 to 7 months — built around the science of early exposure, not just play.

Why age 4–7 months

The window that shapes a lifetime

Early, positive exposure to new textures, sounds, smells and sights during this window helps prevent fear and anxiety later in life, and builds the confidence and resilience that carries into adulthood — alongside the social skills and friendships that start right here.

Before you book

Common questions

Why is it limited to 4–7 months old?

That window is when early, positive sensory exposure has the most lasting effect — helping prevent fear and anxiety and building confidence that carries into adulthood.

Can I bring my puppy's favorite toy?

No — toys and outside food aren't allowed in the sensory garden, so the focus stays on exploring the space itself.

How often does this run?

Once a month, every 3rd Saturday. Slots are limited to 2 dogs, so it's worth booking ahead.

My puppy just turned 8 months — can they still come?

Puppy Play Date is for the 4–7 month window specifically. Socialization 101, part of the Sensory Garden's Shared Slots, picks up from 7 months onward.
