e-Permits

e-Permit — Sensory Garden

Please read the points below to come well prepared for your visit.

  • Please arrive on time — we may not be able to extend your slot if the next one is already booked. You're welcome to keep your dog on leash to explore a section of the park while you wait.
  • Bring a towel and a comb for your fur-kid. If you plan to wade into the water with your dog, we recommend a change of clothes — the water feature is 2.5 feet deep.
  • The Sensory Garden is designed to encourage interaction with natural surroundings, so toys, training aids, frisbees, and other interactive objects aren't allowed inside.
  • Food (except dry dog treats) isn't allowed inside the park. Food should be eaten in the kitchen area or the sit-out only.
  • Bring dry, preferably grain-free treats — small pieces your dog can't resist — since some activities may require them and your dog may get hungry.
  • Drinking water bottles can be refilled on-site.
  • We run a "No Carbon Footprint" policy — please make sure poop and any plastics are removed and placed in the appropriate bins.

e-Permit — Workspace

For the humans

  • You're welcome from 8:30am through 12:30pm (if a call runs past 12:30pm, feel free to finish it before logging off).
  • Scentral Park provides Wi-Fi, seating, and power sockets — bring your own work equipment.
  • Tea and coffee are self-serve in the sit-out area during breaks. No food or snacks are allowed in the sensory garden.
  • Work from our space while your furkid enjoys the garden — as in a Shared Slot, up to 5 compatible dogs will share the space with our team keeping a broad eye on them. We reserve the right to separate dogs if any situation could put them at risk.
  • With your consent, work and play may be photographed or filmed at a discreet distance for promotional use.

For the dogs

  • This is a sensory garden designed for dogs to interact with their natural environment — no toys, training tools, frisbees, or other interactive objects allowed inside.
  • Our team may move a dog out of the park if they become overstimulated, to avoid stressing other dogs in the slot.
  • Food (except dry treats) isn't allowed inside — please eat in the kitchen area or sit-out only.
  • Bring dry, grain-free treats, a towel, and a comb. Drinking water bottles can be refilled on-site.
  • Please help us maintain our "No Carbon Footprint" policy by disposing of poop and plastics in the bins provided.

We look forward to hosting you!
