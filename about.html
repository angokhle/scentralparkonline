Founded in 2024, by dog people.

Purpose & goals

Why we started

What began as a small, local dog park has grown into a full canine center. Along the way, the goals stayed the same:

  • Foster a safe, stimulating environment for dogs to explore and learn
  • Provide a supportive community for dog owners
  • Promote canine wellbeing through thoughtful services and facilities
  • Educate pet parents on why sensory experience matters

It's how we live.

Between us, Vidya and Anand share our home with six dogs, and we look after two more who live with us on the farm. Every wagging tail, every muddy paw print, every 6 AM wake-up call has taught us something about what dogs actually need to feel safe, happy, and free. Scentral Park was born from our slightly obsessive love for dogs.

By day, we're both in IT, juggling day jobs like everyone else. We put our free time and the money into this — because we believe dogs deserve a place that's actually built around them.

Along the way, we needed someone who could turn passion into daily reality — someone to actually run the place, day in and day out. That's Devisri, a canine behaviorist for a decade now and our Managing Partner.

Devisri didn't just come in to manage operations. She came in with ideas and a vision for what a sensory garden could be — how dogs could explore, sniff, and experience the space in ways that mattered to them.

Why "Scentral Park"?

Say it out loud. Go on. "Scent"ral Park.

That's the whole secret, hiding in plain sight — because for a dog, scent is the world. It's how they read a room, remember a friend, fall in love with a patch of grass for absolutely no reason a human will ever understand. We didn't want a park that just looked good. We wanted one that smelled like an adventure, sniff by sniff, corner by corner. So the name had to carry that in it — not just a park, a scentral park.

And there's a little wink to Central Perk in there too. That scruffy, sofa-shaped coffee house where everybody just... showed up. Just friends being friends. That's the feeling we wanted Scentral Park to have — except swap the coffee mugs for water bowls, and the sofa for a sunny patch of grass or sand. A place where dogs come together the way old friends do. Where tails do the talking. Where "how YOU doin'?" is just a good long sniff hello.

So really, Scentral Park is two ideas wearing one name: a park built entirely around scent — the sense dogs live and love by — and a gathering place built entirely around friendship, the way dogs (and honestly, their humans too) do it best.

Come for the nose. Stay for the pack.

Common questions

About the park

What services does the park offer overall?

Boarding, the sensory garden, a workspace, dog socialization programs, behaviour consultation, and rehabilitation services.

Can I visit for the day without boarding?

Yes — day visits let your dog enjoy the facilities and socialization programs without an overnight stay.

What does behaviour consultation include?

Working one-on-one with a professional to address specific behavioural issues, with strategies and training techniques tailored to your dog.
