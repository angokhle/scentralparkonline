The park's journey

Founded in 2024, by dog people.

Born from a simple idea — a space where dogs could engage every sense while their humans got real peace of mind.

Purpose & goals

Why we started

What began as a small, local dog park has grown into a full canine center. Along the way, the goals stayed the same:

  • Foster a safe, stimulating environment for dogs to explore and learn
  • Provide a supportive community for dog owners
  • Promote canine wellbeing through thoughtful services and facilities
  • Educate pet parents on why sensory experience matters

Common questions

About the park

What services does the park offer overall?

Boarding, the sensory garden, a workspace, dog socialization programs, behaviour consultation, and rehabilitation services.

Can I visit for the day without boarding?

Yes — day visits let your dog enjoy the facilities and socialization programs without an overnight stay.

What does behaviour consultation include?

Working one-on-one with a professional to address specific behavioural issues, with strategies and training techniques tailored to your dog.
