Before you visit

Common questions

Do I need to book through the app?

Yes — booking is exclusively through The Scentral Park app. It's the only way to reserve a slot for the sensory garden, boarding, grooming or a puppy play date.

Do I need to stay with my dog during a visit?

Yes, one human companion is required in the sensory garden for the full slot.

What is a sensory garden?

A scientifically designed space with water, sand, wood and plant textures that stimulate a dog's senses and support wellbeing.
